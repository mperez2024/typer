LogR

The Honda LogR™ app is designed to help Type R drivers achieve significant improvements in lap times in a closed-course setting.

  • Unlike other datalogging apps, Honda LogR gains exclusive access to the onboard computer and sensors of the Type R to monitor a variety of performance parameters.
  • The app must be downloaded to a compatible smartphone that’s been paired to the vehicle and plugged into the USB Smartphone/Audio Interface.

Also available after the smartphone has been unplugged is Auto Score, a function that monitors driver behavior over time and generates a score against a baseline established by professional drivers to encourage smooth use of controls and improve skills.
